My UI/UX settlement

Design is how I tell my story.

I used to build stories through physical spaces thinking about how people move, interact, and experience a place.

Today, I’m building those stories in a different space: digital experiences.

My approach to design has always been about more than how something looks. It’s about how it feels, how it works, and what it makes people do.

From drawing the first line to building the final experience, I’m always looking for the connection between people, problems and possibilities.

Different space. Same designer.

Just a new island to build.

About me

Welcome to my island.

I’m Sakshi, and my settlements are firmly placed in UI/UX Design on one side, Architecture on the other. Since they’re neighbors on my board, I get to build on both and if there’s one thing I’ll always chase in my island, it’s the Longest Road.
