Travello, wrapped up

What this project is about

Travello is an all-in-one companion for solo travelers. Instead of juggling maps, translators, itineraries, and safety tools across separate apps, it unifies planning, navigation, local context, and emergency support into one calm mobile experience.

  • 8+Core journeys designed
  • 3Research personas
  • 1Unified travel toolkit
  • 100%Mobile-first system

Design principles

  • One journey, not ten apps

    Every feature earns its place by reducing tool-switching during real travel moments.

  • Safety without panic

    Emergency and awareness tools stay reachable, never theatrical or fear-driven.

  • Local context first

    Etiquette, language, and place cues appear when they help—not as decoration.

  • Clarity under motion

    Interfaces stay scannable on the go: bold hierarchy, short labels, decisive actions.

Design hierarchy

  1. 01Primary action

    The next useful step—navigate, translate, SOS—always reads first.

  2. 02Situational context

    Time, place, and trip state sit under the action without competing with it.

  3. 03Supporting detail

    Tips, history, and secondary tools stay available but visually quieter.

  4. 04System chrome

    Navigation and account live at the edges so content can own the center.

Travello’s design system keeps solo travel oriented: fewer decisions in the moment, clearer priorities on every screen.
