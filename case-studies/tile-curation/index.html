Marketing the 30 tiles

How these tiles go to market

This is not a brand campaign for tiles in general. It is a plan for the thirty SKUs in this catalogue: Travertino, Oak and Walnut, subway and KitKat in four colours, pill and bevelled tactiles, burgundy, Moroccan and motif patterns, Tropical and Botanical, marble and burgundy marble, anti-skid floors, Bellagio panels, and the terrazzos. One fact does most of the selling: sage, terracotta, dusty blue and beige run through wall, floor and texture, so a customer who picks a colour already has a room.

Campaign ideas

  • Pick a colour. We shall do the rest. The line for the whole thirty. Sage, terracotta, dusty blue or beige: subway, KitKat, pill, anti-skid and Bellagio already exist in that colour. A three-question quiz returns a thread, four tiles and a sample box.
  • After the Apple Store. Drawn from the curation argument: ten years of plain walls. The campaign pairs a grey apartment bathroom with the same room in Travertino, then in sage KitKat, then in burgundy marble. The thirty are the alternative, not an addition.
  • Prompt to Product. Users already typed “minimal and earthy with pastels” and “kids bathroom in blue”. Show the AI image beside a room built only from these tiles: sage subway + Travertino; dusty blue KitKat + white subway + anti-skid blue.
  • Launch as six drops. One edition a week. Softness first (the easiest sale), burgundy last (the talking point). The other twenty-four stay in the wings until their week.

Designer and architect communication

  • Spec Kit of the thirty only. 150×150 chips, divided by edition, laid as rooms not as a colour chart. Grout card included. Oak next to terracotta subway. Charcoal Bellagio next to Botanical. The box should open looking like a mood board.
  • Texture maps for the tiles that need them. Seamless files and normal maps for pill, bevelled, KitKat and all three Bellagio profiles. Tropical and Botanical as tileable 600×1200 maps so the print continues in Enscape. Designers specify what they can render.
  • One board per recommended combination, as a Figma and PDF, client-presentable as it is. Same eight rooms as the website.
  • Technical sheet per SKU: PEI, slip rating for the four anti-skids, water absorption, rectified or not, shade variation, lead time. Burgundy marble and Travertino need shade variation called out in writing.
  • A short session at the centre: “How to specify the Bellagio and the pill so the shadow survives the site.” Trade pricing and samples to site with a named contact.

Helping them choose quickly

  • Pick the colour first. Sage, terracotta, dusty blue or beige. Two thirds of the thirty step aside. What remains already matches: subway, KitKat, pill, anti-skid, Bellagio.
  • Then pick the room. Bathroom floor must be Travertino, terrazzo, Diamond White or anti-skid. Kitchen splashback may be subway, KitKat, marble or Diamond White. Statement prints (Tropical, Botanical, motif, Moroccan) are one wall only.
  • The four-tile room. Floor, quiet wall, texture, statement. Statement is optional. Printed on the hub, on every panel and in the quiz result.
  • Busyness score, 1 to 5. Travertino and marble are 1. KitKat and pill are 3. Tropical, Botanical and motif are 5. Keep a room at 6 or under. Two patterns do not share a room in this collection.
  • Space badges on every chip: bathroom wall, bathroom floor, kitchen splashback, wet area, anti-skid, small-room friendly. The customer filters themselves.
  • Kitchen stays quieter than the bath. Cabinets and counters are already competing. Default kitchen: Oak or Walnut + terracotta or beige subway. Save Tropical and burgundy marble for baths and powder rooms, unless the kitchen is the feature.
